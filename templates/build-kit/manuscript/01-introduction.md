# Introduction {#introduction}

Chronic venous disease is common in adults, and venous leg ulcer is its most disabling complication. Ulcers recur often and impose a large burden on patients and health services.

Statins have anti-inflammatory effects that could act on the venous wall, but the clinical evidence is limited to small studies with short follow-up, and no prospective cohort has examined incident ulcer as the primary outcome.

We aimed to estimate the association between statin use at baseline and incident venous leg ulcer over 5 years in adults with chronic venous disease. This report follows the Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology (STROBE) statement [@vonelm2007strengthening; @vandenbroucke2007strengthening].
