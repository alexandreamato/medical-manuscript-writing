# Results {#results}

Of 2140 potentially eligible patients, 1500 were included; 620 used statins and 880 did not (@fig:flow). Baseline characteristics are shown in @tbl:baseline.

A venous leg ulcer occurred in 41 statin users and 88 non-users. After adjustment, statin use was associated with a lower hazard of ulcer (HR 0.72, 95% CI 0.55 to 0.94; @tbl:outcome).

::: {#tbl:baseline}
Table: Baseline characteristics by statin use.

| Characteristic        | Statin users (n = 620) | Non-users (n = 880) |
|:----------------------|-----------------------:|--------------------:|
| Age, years, mean (SD) | 63.1 (8.2)             | 58.4 (9.0)          |
| Women, n (%)          | 402 (64.8)             | 611 (69.4)          |
| CEAP C4, n (%)        | 118 (19.0)             | 149 (16.9)          |
:::

::: {#tbl:outcome}
Table: Incident venous leg ulcer by statin use.

| Group        | Events | HR (95% CI), adjusted |
|:-------------|-------:|----------------------:|
| Non-users    | 88     | 1 (reference)         |
| Statin users | 41     | 0.72 (0.55 to 0.94)   |
:::
