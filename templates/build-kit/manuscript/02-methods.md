# Methods {#methods}

## Study design and setting {#methods-design}

This was a prospective cohort study at one outpatient vascular clinic. Participants were recruited from 2018 to 2020 and followed for 5 years.

## Participants {#methods-participants}

Adults aged 40 years or older with chronic venous disease classified by Clinical, Etiological, Anatomical, and Pathophysiological (CEAP) class as C2 to C4 were eligible. The flow of participants is shown in @fig:flow.

## Exposure and outcome {#methods-variables}

The exposure was statin use recorded at baseline. The primary outcome was the first venous leg ulcer confirmed by a vascular surgeon.

## Statistical analysis {#methods-statistics}

Hazard ratios (HRs) with 95% CIs were estimated with Cox proportional hazards regression, adjusted for age, sex, body mass index (BMI), and CEAP class. Residual confounding was assessed with the E-value [@vanderweele2017sensitivity].
