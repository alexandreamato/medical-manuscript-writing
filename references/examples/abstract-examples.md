# Abstract Examples Index (Medical Research)

All abstract example cites point to the local files below. Each template includes a worked medical example.

1. Template A: randomized controlled trial (CONSORT).
   `Gap in the evidence, the trial that answers it, the primary result with absolute and relative effect and 95% CI plus harms, and a causal conclusion limited to the trial population.`
   `references/examples/abstract/template-a.md`

2. Template B: observational study, target trial emulation, or Mendelian randomization (STROBE / STROBE-MR).
   `Gap in the evidence, the design and confounding strategy that address it, the adjusted association with 95% CI and a sensitivity analysis, and a conclusion stated as an association.`
   `references/examples/abstract/template-b.md`

3. Template C: systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020).
   `Why a synthesis is needed now, the review methods, the pooled effect with heterogeneity, prespecified subgroups or harms, and GRADE certainty, with a conclusion as strong as the certainty allows.`
   `references/examples/abstract/template-c.md`
