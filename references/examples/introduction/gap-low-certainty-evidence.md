# Gap: Evidence Exists but Its Certainty Is Low

`Use when the question has been studied, but only in designs or sizes that cannot support a confident answer (observational data only, small or short trials, surrogate outcomes, high risk of bias).`

## Skeleton

```
% State what the existing evidence suggests
[Observational studies / small trials] have reported that [INTERVENTION / EXPOSURE]
is associated with [OUTCOME].

% State the specific reason the certainty is low
However, [REASON: confounding by indication / small samples with wide confidence intervals /
surrogate outcomes / short follow-up / high risk of bias], so [what cannot be concluded].

% Anchor with a synthesis or guideline grading if available
A recent [systematic review / guideline] rated the certainty of this evidence as [low / very low].

% State what design would resolve it
Whether [INTERVENTION] [changes OUTCOME] in [POPULATION] therefore requires [DESIGN FEATURE:
randomization / adequate power / patient-important outcome / longer follow-up].
```

## Worked example (illustrative; fictional)

```
Observational studies have reported that early mobilization after
catheter-directed thrombolysis for iliofemoral deep vein thrombosis is associated with
lower rates of post-thrombotic syndrome. However, patients mobilized early were younger
and had less extensive thrombus, so the association may reflect prognosis rather than
the effect of mobilization. The two randomized trials available enrolled 48 and 62
patients, followed them for 6 months, and reported venous patency rather than
post-thrombotic syndrome. A recent systematic review rated the certainty of the evidence
as very low. Whether early mobilization reduces post-thrombotic syndrome at 24 months
therefore requires an adequately powered randomized comparison with a patient-important
outcome.
```

## Notes and pitfalls

1. Name the mechanism of uncertainty (confounding, imprecision, indirectness, bias). "Previous studies were limited" gives the reader nothing to evaluate.
2. The design you propose in the closing paragraph must fix the specific weakness you named here; otherwise the gap and the study do not match.
3. Cite the certainty grading (for example, GRADE in a systematic review or guideline) only if it exists; do not grade the evidence yourself in the Introduction as if it were an established rating.
