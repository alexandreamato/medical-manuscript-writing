# Results: Participant Flow Paragraph

`Use as the first paragraph of the Results. It gives the numbers every later analysis must agree with, and cites the flow diagram (Figure 1).`

All worked examples in this folder use one fictional randomized trial: 600 adults with venous leg ulcer randomized to supervised exercise plus compression (n = 300) or compression alone (n = 300); primary outcome complete healing at 24 weeks.

## Skeleton

```
% Recruitment period and screening
From [MONTH YEAR] to [MONTH YEAR], [N] [patients] were assessed for eligibility and
[N] were excluded ([reason 1, n]; [reason 2, n]; [reason 3, n]).

% Randomized / included and allocation
[N] were randomized: [n] to [ARM A] and [n] to [ARM B] (Figure 1).

% Follow-up and analysis populations
[n] and [n] completed [FOLLOW-UP]; [n] and [n] were lost to follow-up. All randomized
participants were included in the primary [intention-to-treat] analysis.
```

## Worked example (illustrative; fictional)

```
From March 2022 to August 2024, 812 patients were assessed for eligibility and 212
were excluded (148 did not meet the inclusion criteria, 51 declined to participate,
and 13 for other reasons). Of the 600 patients randomized, 300 were assigned to
supervised exercise plus compression and 300 to compression alone (Figure 1). The
24-week assessment was completed by 291 and 289 patients; 9 and 11 were lost to
follow-up. All 600 randomized patients were included in the intention-to-treat analysis.
```

## Notes and pitfalls

1. The exclusion reasons must add up to the excluded total, and every later denominator must be traceable to these numbers.
2. State the analysis population here so readers know what "n" means in Table 2.
3. Recruitment dates belong here or in Methods, not in both with different values.
